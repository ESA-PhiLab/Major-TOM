# ERA5Land-Monthly

Monthly land-surface state for every land cell of the Major TOM grid, 1950–2025: 49 ERA5-Land variables (temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, precipitation, radiation and heat fluxes, soil water and temperature, snow, runoff, evaporation, lakes, vegetation), with 1991–2020 climate normals. Part of [Major TOM Climate](https://source.coop/major-tom/climate).

## Variables

Every time-varying variable ERA5-Land offers as a monthly mean. Columns are `era5land_` and ERA5-Land's short name; units are as the Climate Data Store gives monthly means: **accumulated variables (precipitation, snowfall, evaporation, runoff, radiation and heat fluxes, snowmelt) are per day**, the mean daily total of the month. `era5land_valid` is the share of the cell covered by source pixels with data.

## Files

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `cells.parquet` | `grid_cell`, `grid_row_u`, `grid_col_r` |
| `series/year=YYYY/MM.parquet` | one row per cell, along the Hilbert curve: `grid_cell`, `grid_row_u`, `grid_col_r`, `year`, `month`, the columns above |
| `climatology/band=NN.parquet` | one row per cell and calendar month, 1991–2020: `grid_cell`, `grid_row_u`, `grid_col_r`, `month`, `<column>_mean`, `<column>_std`, `n_years` |
| `by_cell/band=NN.parquet` | every month of each cell together: rows by cell (along the Hilbert curve), then `year`, `month`; the same columns as `series/`; row groups of 1,024 cells × 912 months, with a page index ([layout](https://source.coop/major-tom/climate#file-layout)) |
| `overviews/year=YYYY/MM.parquet` | one row per block of 4 × 4 cells with a value, for maps of the whole globe: `block_row` = (`grid_row_u` + 1002) // 4, `block_col` = (`grid_col_r` + 2004) // 4, `n_cells`, each column's mean |
| `climatology_overviews/MM.parquet` | one row per block of 4 × 4 cells with a value and calendar month MM: `block_row`, `block_col`, `n_cells`, and the block mean of each `<column>_mean` of the climatology |

Which files to read: `series/` for one month over many cells; `by_cell/` for one place over time; `overviews/` for a view of the whole globe from one small file. `series/` and `by_cell/` hold the same values.

```python
import fsspec
import pandas as pd

https = fsspec.filesystem("https")      # reads only the parts of a file a query needs
url = "https://data.source.coop/major-tom/climate/ERA5Land-Monthly"
here = [("grid_row_u", "=", 624), ("grid_col_r", "=", -29)]                     # Loch Lomond, 624U_29L
july = pd.read_parquet(f"{url}/series/year=2024/07.parquet", filesystem=https, columns=["grid_cell", "era5land_t2m", "era5land_swvl1"])
normals = pd.read_parquet(f"{url}/climatology/band=16.parquet", filesystem=https, filters=here,
                          columns=["grid_cell", "month", "era5land_t2m_mean", "era5land_t2m_std"])          # 12 months
history = pd.read_parquet(f"{url}/by_cell/band=16.parquet", filesystem=https, filters=here,
                          columns=["year", "month", "era5land_tp", "era5land_swvl1"])                      # 912 months
```

The band of a cell is (row + 1002) × 20 // 2004, with row the signed number in its name (`624U_29L`: 624, band 16). Filter on `grid_row_u` and `grid_col_r`, the numbers in a cell's name (U and R positive, D and L negative): the row-group statistics then let a reader skip the rest of the file. A filter on `grid_cell` reads the whole file.

## Notes

- **Units of accumulations.** Monthly means of accumulated variables are mean daily totals: `era5land_tp` in metres per day (× 1000 × days in the month for a monthly total in mm), `era5land_ssrd` in J m⁻² per day. Fluxes are positive downwards, so evaporation (`era5land_e`, `era5land_pev` and the `eva*` terms) is negative.
- **Land only.** ERA5-Land has values over land and inland water only; coastal cells are means over their land pixels, and `era5land_valid` gives the covered share.
- **Lake variables.** `era5land_lblt`, `licd`, `lict`, `lmld`, `lmlt`, `ltlt` describe the lake model (bottom, ice, mixed layer, total layer). Where a cell has no lake, the model still carries values for its lake tile; use them with ERA5-Static's lake cover (`era5_cl`).
- **ERA5-Land and ERA5.** ERA5-Land does not assimilate observations itself: it is forced by ERA5's atmosphere. Its precipitation is ERA5's, interpolated to 0.1°; its temperatures differ from ERA5's mainly in mountains, through the terrain adjustment.
- **Climatology period.** 1991–2020, the current WMO normal period.
- **Cell means.** Values are area-weighted means over each cell, without interpolation; see the [top-level README](https://source.coop/major-tom/climate).
